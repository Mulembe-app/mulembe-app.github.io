Mulembe 1.1.0

  • Plans from $2.99 a month: Personal for you, Team with a seat for each person. Subscribe opens the plans on the website.
  • Next Phase: one click asks Claude for the next phase's prompt and starts it in a new pane next to this one, with the model and effort it names. Phases can also run automatically, one after another, each committing its own work.
  • Phase panes are numbered after the pane they came from (5-1, 5-2), and Close Sub-Panes closes them all at once.
  • Click [Image #N] to see the screenshot you pasted. Pinch, or press Control + and Control −, to zoom.
  • Copying from a pane gives you the full text of a long paste instead of [Pasted text #1].
  • Stop a local server from its 🌐 pill. Servers left running by sessions that ended stop once nothing uses them, like Docker of a project you leave.
  • The 📱 pill shows your project's app in an iOS simulator, and simulators agents leave running shut down by themselves.
  • Parked panes free Claude's memory and pick up the same conversation when you come back. You can also keep working with the lid closed.
  • In Claude's input field, ⌘A and ⌘⇧ with the arrows select your message like in Notes, and ⌘Z brings back deleted text.
  • The folder chooser fits small panes, and the menu bar icon comes back after display changes.
  • Many fixes for reliability, speed and battery life.
