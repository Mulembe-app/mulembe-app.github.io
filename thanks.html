Welcome to Mulembe.

Your subscription is active. Three small steps, and every agent has its calm window.

  1. Find your license key. Open the receipt Polar emailed you and follow the link to your customer portal. Your license key is there, ready to copy.

  2. Open Mulembe and choose License. You'll find it in the Mulembe menu, and in the menu bar icon's menu as Enter License Key.

  3. Paste the key and click Activate. That's it. Your license works on up to 3 of your Macs, and updates install by themselves.

Bought for your team?

In the same customer portal, assign a seat to each colleague by email. Each of them gets an invitation and their own license key, and follows the same three steps.
