Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

The app

Mulembe has no analytics, no tracking and no ads. Your settings, layouts and notes stay on your Mac. Voice input is recognized on your Mac. Sunset and sunrise for the automatic theme are worked out from your time zone, so the app never asks for your location.

The app goes online for two things only:

  • Your license. To activate and occasionally check your license key, it sends the key and your Mac's name to Polar (polar.sh), which runs our store. Your Mac's name is shown to you in the Polar customer portal, so you can see and manage your activations.
  • Updates. It checks this website for a new version of Mulembe, using the open-source Sparkle updater. That request contains no personal data beyond what any web request includes, such as your IP address, and it goes to GitHub Pages, which hosts this website.

The programs you run inside Mulembe, such as Claude Code or Codex, connect to their own services under their own terms and privacy policies. Mulembe doesn't send them anything on its own.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Polar as the merchant of record, which means Polar sells Mulembe to you on our behalf and processes your payment. Polar handles your payment details under its own privacy policy. We receive your order details, such as your email address, country and what you bought, so we can provide your license and support. The legal basis is the performance of our contract with you.

Support

If you email us, we use your message and email address to help you and keep the conversation only as long as it is useful for that.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address to keep the service secure, as described in the GitHub privacy statement. The website sets no cookies and uses no analytics.

Transfers outside the EU

Polar and GitHub may process data outside the European Economic Area. They rely on safeguards recognised under the GDPR, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Who is responsible

Mulembe is run by Artur Pereć in Poland, who is responsible for your data under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). You can reach us at mulembeapp@icloud.com.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to withdraw consent at any time. Just email us at mulembeapp@icloud.com. You can also complain to your data protection authority. In Poland that is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO).
